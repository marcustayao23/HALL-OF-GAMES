How to use Hall of Games

  • Browse games: Select Browse to see all games, or use Categories to filter by genre.
  • Find a game: Select Search, type a title, and press Enter on Home or browse the live results on Browse.
  • Use the carousel: Scroll over it, click and hold then drag left or right, or focus it and use the left and right arrow keys.
  • Open a game: Click a carousel image to open that game's details. Other games are hidden until you select Browse in the navigation.
