FORTNITE

Fortnite is a fast-paced battle royale game where 100 players drop onto a constantly shrinking island to scavenge for weapons, build structures, and outlast everyone else to be the last one standing.

Tags: Battle Royale, Survival, Shooter, Multiplayer, Building, Action, Co-op

Developer & Publisher: Developed and published by Epic Games.

VALORANT

Valorant is a tactical 5v5 character-based shooter where precise gunplay meets unique agent abilities, requiring tight team coordination and strategic strategy to plant or defuse the Spike.

Tags: Tactical Shooter, Hero Shooter, FPS, Competitive, Multiplayer

Developer & Publisher: Developed and published by Riot Games.
